*Last updated: October 7, 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how and why the Maximal Intelligence Corporation ("Maximal Intelligence", "we", "us", or "our") accesses, collects, stores, uses, and/or discloses your personal information when you use the Maximal Intelligence services ("Services"), including when you:

## 1. What Personal Data Does Maximal Intelligence Collect? <span id="collect"/>

### Information You Provide Directly Is Collected

For example:

1.  *Account Information:* When you request to register for or log in to Maximal Intelligence, we collect your email, credentials, and other personal information you choose to provide. Your account information may be stored in logs to prevent misuse of the Services. If Maximal Intelligence identifies misuse associated with your account, you may be denied future access.

2.  *Contact Information:* When you email support or submit the contact form on our website, we store the information you provide as contact information. For the contact form this is your name, email, affiliation (optional), and message (optional).

3.  *Newsletter Information:* When you sign up for updates on our website, we collect the email address you submit. The newsletter is not currently being sent; we will only email you if you have signed up.

4.  *Content You Provide Through Use Of The Services:* When you submit information to the Services, that information gets logged and associated with your Personal data. It is used to identify technical issues and keep an audit trail of the use of Maximal Intelligence services.

### Information About Your Use Of The Services We Collect Automatically

We may collect certain Personal Data automatically through your use of the Services, for example:

1.  *Device Information And Identifiers:* When you access and use our Services, we automatically collect information about your device, which may include: device type, your device operating system, browser type and version, language preference, IP address, hardware identifiers, and mobile IDs. We may also derive your approximate location from this information, including country, city, state and postal code. This information may be collected through any use of the Services.

2.  *Website Usage Data:* When you visit our Websites, we automatically log information about how you interact with the sites, such as the date and time of visit, the requests you submitted, and the pages you have viewed or links you have clicked.

3.  *Cookies And Similar Tracking Technologies:* Maximal Intelligence uses cookies and similar technologies to provide functionality, such as storing your session, and to recognize you as you use our Services.

4.  *Third-Party Integrations:* The Services may contain buttons, links, tools and content from third-party services, such as LinkedIn. However, any third-party integrations' policies and procedures are not controlled by Maximal Intelligence, and this Privacy Statement does not cover how third-party integrations use your information. We recommend you read the privacy statements of any third-party companies before connecting to or using their applications or services.

5.  *Version Data And Error Tracking:* We automatically check for the version of the software you are running, along with accompanying data such as hostname, HTTP Agent, Header, and IP address. These version checks help ensure your instance is up-to-date with the latest security patches and features. We also collect error trace data and connect that data to account data so that we can diagnose and resolve technical issues.

## 2. What Personal Data Is Not Collected By Maximal Intelligence?

We do not collect Personal Data related to race, ethnicity, political opinions, religion, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data, health data and sexual orientation.

Additionally, California law requires reporting on web browser DNT signals, which are not standardized. Maximal Intelligence does not implement DNT signals into website behavior.

Because Maximal Intelligence does not sell personal information or share personal information for targeted advertising, there is no opt-out preference signal (such as Global Privacy Control) that would apply to our processing. If our practices change in the future, we will honor applicable opt-out signals as required by law.

Further, Maximal Intelligence does not knowingly collect Personal Data from, or direct any of our Services to, children under the age of 13. If we learn or have reason to believe that a user is under 13, we will promptly close that account and delete associated Personal Data in accordance with applicable law. By using the Services, you represent that you are at least 13 or the equivalent age as specified by law in your jurisdiction.

## 8. Data Retention <span id="retention"/>

In order to protect your Personal Data, Maximal Intelligence will only retain your Personal Data for as long as your account is active or as needed to perform our contractual obligations, provide you the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, preserve legal rights, or enforce our agreements.

Specifically, we apply the following criteria:

-   *Account Information and Content You Provide Through Use Of The Services:* retained while your account is active, and afterwards only as long as needed to meet legal obligations and prevent misuse of the Services.
-   *Contact Information:* contact form submissions and support emails are retained as long as needed to handle your inquiry, plus a reasonable follow-up period.
-   *Newsletter Information:* retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.
-   *Automatically collected data* (Device Information And Identifiers, Website Usage Data, Version Data And Error Tracking): retained only as long as needed for security, abuse prevention, diagnosing technical issues, and to fulfill any legal obligations.

When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will either delete or anonymize such information, or, if this is not possible (for example, because your personal information has been stored in backup archives), then we will securely store your personal information and isolate it from any further processing until deletion is possible.

## 9. What Are Your Rights And Choices Regarding Personal Data? <span id="rights"/>

You have the right to access, correct, restrict, delete, or receive a portable copy of your Personal Data, to object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to withdraw any consent you have given. While these rights may vary by jurisdiction, Maximal Intelligence provides you with the same rights and choices, no matter where you live. We provide these rights free of charge. Maximal Intelligence reserves the right to decline data subject rights requests that are manifestly unfounded, repetitive, or abusive in nature, or where fulfilling the request would conflict with our legitimate interests in enforcing our terms, policies, or legal rights, including where an account has been suspended or terminated for violation of our terms or other applicable policies.

You may exercise your choices and rights as follows:

*Request a copy of your information:* You may request a copy of the Personal Data stored on our servers, including in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, by emailing support@maximalintelligence.com.

*Update your information:* If you have an account, you may update your account information or log out from your [account management page](https://cloud.maximalintelligence.com/account/settings).

*Object to processing:* You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests by emailing support@maximalintelligence.com. We will stop that processing unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing is needed for legal claims.

*Withdraw consent:* You may unsubscribe from newsletters at any time using the unsubscribe link in any newsletter or by emailing support@maximalintelligence.com.

*To delete your account and information:* If you want to delete your account or information you may do so by emailing support@maximalintelligence.com. Note that Maximal Intelligence keeps logs that may be necessary to perform our legal duties and prevent misuse of the service. In such a case, the account information will be deleted after it has been determined that all legal obligations have been fulfilled.

*To inform your Supervisory Authority:* In the unlikely event you disagree with our handling of your request, you have the right to file a complaint with the competent supervisory authority in your jurisdiction.

If you are located in the EEA or UK and you believe we are unlawfully processing your personal information, you may complain to your [Member State data protection authority](https://ec.europa.eu/justice/data-protection/bodies/authorities/index_en.htm) or [UK data protection authority](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/data-protection-complaints/data-protection-complaints/).

If you are located in Switzerland, you may contact the [Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner](https://www.edoeb.admin.ch/edoeb/en/home.html).

*Appeals:* If you are located in a state or country where you are granted the right to appeal our resolution of a data subject rights request, we will provide the specific information on how to appeal in our initial resolution email to your request.
